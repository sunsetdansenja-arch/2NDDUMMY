Berdiri sejak 2011, Adon telah melatih ratusan peselancar lokal maupun mancanegara dengan pemahaman mendalam tentang karakter arus laut Aceh. Established in 2011, Adon has coached hundreds of domestic and international surfers with an intimate understanding of Aceh's wave dynamics.
