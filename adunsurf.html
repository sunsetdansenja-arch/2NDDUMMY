Est. 2011 (15+ Years)

Berdiri sejak 2011, Adon telah melatih ratusan peselancar lokal maupun mancanegara dengan pemahaman mendalam tentang karakter arus laut Aceh. Established in 2011, Adon has coached hundreds of domestic and international surfers with an intimate knowledge of Aceh's ocean currents.

1-on-1 Guidance

Pendampingan penuh secara personal. Adon berada langsung di samping Anda di dalam air untuk memastikan keamanan dan kemajuan teknik secara presisi. Full 1-on-1 personal guidance. Adon stays right beside you in the water, ensuring maximum safety and precise technique progression.

All Gear Provided

Anda tidak perlu membawa peralatan sendiri. Papan selancar profesional, rashguard, dan perlengkapan keamanan sudah disediakan lengkap. No need to bring your own equipment. Soft-top surfboards, rashguards, and safety gear are fully provided for every session.
